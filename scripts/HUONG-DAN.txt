AD USER MANAGER - HUONG DAN CAI DAT NHANH
=========================================

1. Copy ca thu muc nay len may chu AD (Domain Controller).
2. Double-click install.bat (script tu xin quyen Administrator).
3. Mo trinh duyet: https://<ten-may-chu>:5443
   Dang nhap bang tai khoan AD thuoc nhom Administrators / Domain Admins.

- Chung chi HTTPS tu ky -> trinh duyet canh bao, bam "Advanced" -> "Proceed".
- Doi cong:      mo cmd (Admin) chay: install.bat -Port 8443
- Nang cap:      giai nen ban moi, chay lai install.bat (giu nguyen cau hinh + du lieu).
- Go cai dat:    uninstall.bat   (giu du lieu)
- Thu muc cai:   C:\Program Files\ADUserManager
- Cau hinh:      C:\Program Files\ADUserManager\appsettings.json  (sua xong: restart service ADUserManager)
- Du lieu:       C:\Program Files\ADUserManager\data\adusermanager.db
- Log loi:       Event Viewer -> Windows Logs -> Application, nguon "ADUserManager"
